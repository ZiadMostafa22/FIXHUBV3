# Fix-Hub: Project References

This document provides a list of academic, technical, and professional references you can use in your documentation (Chapter 2: Background or Bibliography).

## 📱 Mobile Development & Frameworks

*   **Flutter Documentation (Official):** Google. (2024). *Flutter Documentation*. Retrieved from [https://docs.flutter.dev/](https://docs.flutter.dev/)
*   **Dart Programming Language:** Google. (2024). *Dart Language Guide*. Retrieved from [https://dart.dev/guides](https://dart.dev/guides)
*   **MVVM Pattern:** Napitupulu, A. D., & Santoso, N. (2022). *Implementation of MVVM Architecture on Android-Based Application*. International Journal of Engineering and Computer Science.

## ☁️ Cloud Services & Databases

*   **Firebase Cloud Firestore:** Google. (2024). *Cloud Firestore Documentation*. Retrieved from [https://firebase.google.com/docs/firestore](https://firebase.google.com/docs/firestore)
*   **NoSQL Databases:** Chodorow, K. (2013). *MongoDB: The Definitive Guide*. O'Reilly Media. (Good for context on NoSQL concepts used in Firestore).
*   **Cloud Computing:** Mell, P., & Grance, T. (2011). *The NIST Definition of Cloud Computing*. National Institute of Standards and Technology.

## 🤖 Artificial Intelligence & Gemini

*   **Google Gemini AI:** Google DeepMind. (2023). *Gemini: A Family of Highly Capable Multimodal Models*. Technical Report. Retrieved from [https://deepmind.google/technologies/gemini/](https://deepmind.google/technologies/gemini/)
*   **Large Language Models (LLMs):** Vaswani, A., et al. (2017). *Attention Is All You Need*. Advances in Neural Information Processing Systems (NeurIPS). (The foundational paper for Transformer models like Gemini).
*   **Conversational AI:** Jurafsky, D., & Martin, J. H. (2023). *Speech and Language Processing*. Stanford University.

## 🔧 Automotive & Management Systems

*   **Automotive Service Management:** Hillier, V. (2012). *Fundamentals of Motor Vehicle Technology*. Nelson Thornes.
*   **Information Systems in SME:** Alshawi, S., et al. (2011). *Implementation of Management Information Systems in Small and Medium Enterprises*. Journal of Enterprise Information Management.
*   **Inventory Management:** Waters, D. (2003). *Inventory Control and Management*. John Wiley & Sons.

## 🏗️ Software Engineering & UML

*   **UML Modeling:** Fowler, M. (2003). *UML Distilled: A Brief Guide to the Standard Object Modeling Language*. Addison-Wesley Professional.
*   **Software Design Patterns:** Gamma, E., et al. (1994). *Design Patterns: Elements of Reusable Object-Oriented Software*. Addison-Wesley. (The "Gang of Four" book for patterns like Singleton and Facade used in FixHub).

---

### Tips for using references:
1.  **Chapter 2 (Background):** Use the technical references to explain why you chose Flutter and Firebase.
2.  **Chapter 3 (Analysis):** Use the management references to justify the need for automated inventory and financial tracking.
3.  **Bibliography:** Ensure you follow the specific formatting style required by your university (APA, IEEE, Harvard, etc.). Most of the above are in a format similar to **APA**.
