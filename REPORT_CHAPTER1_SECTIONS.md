## 1.7 Contributions
This project introduces several key contributions to the field of automotive service management:
*   **Integrated Digital Ecosystem:** Unlike traditional disparate systems, FixHub provides a unified platform connecting all stakeholders (Customers, Technicians, Cashiers, and Admins) in a real-time synchronized environment.
*   **AI-Driven Support:** Integration of Google Gemini AI to provide 24/7 automated technical support and maintenance advice, significantly reducing the load on human customer service.
*   **Optimized Resource Management:** An automated inventory system that links directly to maintenance tasks, ensuring parts are tracked and reordered efficiently without manual intervention.
*   **Enhanced Financial Transparency:** A structured refund and payment workflow that ensures accountability and provides clear financial auditing for service center owners.
*   **Data-Driven Decision Making:** Through comprehensive analytics on technician performance and revenue trends, the system empowers owners to optimize their business operations.
